The owner authorised drafting meaningful Tamil/English text and proceeding with

That implementation exception does not approve this wording, resolve product

## Basis and scope

Original Aarili wording below derives from product §§24 and 32.2 (TRU-03/04/05/08/13),
§7 (CUS-07/09), COM-07 and architecture §11.5. It is a review proposal, not legal
advice or a claim of compliance with any law. No consent wording was observed in
the bounded Nool research; none of this text is attributed to or copied from Nool.
See [research evidence](Customer_Wearer_REA_Evidence.md),
[design](Customer_Wearer_Design.md) and [ticket packet](Customer_Wearer_Ticket_Packet.md).

The registration slices record identities and purpose-specific evidence. They do
not implement measurements, photos, publication, marketing delivery or customer
access. Mentioning those purposes explains boundaries for later features and does
not activate them. An unrecorded choice remains unknown; it is never a grant.

## Draft service notice

Show the draft badge beside both languages in synthetic review screens. Explain
the selected purpose before recording any decision. The following is proposed
customer-facing copy, subject to final owner, privacy and fluent-Tamil review.

### English

**Your details for tailoring service**

We use your name and any contact details you choose to provide to identify you,
contact you about your tailoring service and keep the relevant service records.
We keep the contact or payer and the person wearing the garment as separate
people. Sharing a phone number or paying for an order does not give access to
another person's measurements or private photos.

You do not need to provide a date of birth, identity document or body photo for a
normal tailoring order. Measurements and private fitting photos, when offered,
are handled under their applicable notice and permission. For a wearer under 18,
we must first record the parent or legal guardian's applicable consent before
storing measurements or photos. We never publish a minor's images or use their
data for marketing targeting.

Marketing, promotional publication and optional private photos have separate
choices. Refusing marketing does not prevent ordinary service. You can ask the
boutique owner to correct or export your details, review deletion, or record a
consent withdrawal. We will track your request and explain what can be removed
and what must be kept, with the reason; withdrawal is not a promise that all
records or previously shared copies disappear immediately.

**Draft information still awaiting approval:** The owner must confirm the named
grievance contact and contact method, retention periods for each record type,
processor/location disclosures and the request-verification procedure before
real-data use. No real customer registration is enabled by this draft.

தொடர்பு விவரங்களையும் பயன்படுத்துகிறோம். தொடர்புகொள்பவர் அல்லது பணம் செலுத்துபவர்,
ஆடையை அணிபவர் ஆகியோரைத் தனித்தனி நபர்களாகப் பதிவு செய்கிறோம். ஒரே தொலைபேசி எண்ணைப்

பார்க்க அனுமதி அளிக்காது.

வழக்கமான தையல் ஆர்டருக்கு பிறந்த தேதி, அடையாள ஆவணம் அல்லது உடல் புகைப்படம் வழங்கத்

அணிபவர் 18 வயதுக்குக் குறைவானவர் என்றால், அவரின் அளவுகள் அல்லது புகைப்படங்களைச்
சேமிப்பதற்கு முன் பெற்றோர் அல்லது சட்டப்பூர்வ பாதுகாவலரின் பொருந்தும் ஒப்புதலைப் பதிவு
செய்ய வேண்டும். சிறார்களின் படங்களை வெளியிடுவதோ, அவர்களின் தரவுகளை இலக்கு

**இன்னும் ஒப்புதல் பெற வேண்டிய வரைவு விவரங்கள்:** உண்மையான வாடிக்கையாளர் தரவுகளைப்

இந்த வரைவு உண்மையான வாடிக்கையாளர் பதிவை அனுமதிக்காது.

## Draft service decision and evidence

English grant wording: “The tailoring-service use described in this notice has
been explained to me. I agree to that stated use for the identified person.”

Tamil grant wording: “இந்த அறிவிப்பில் கூறிய தையல் சேவைப் பயன்பாடு எனக்கு
விளக்கப்பட்டது. குறிப்பிடப்பட்ட நபருக்காக அந்தப் பயன்பாட்டிற்கு ஒப்புக்கொள்கிறேன்.”

Record an explicit `grant`, `refusal` or `withdrawal` for the identified subject
and service purpose. “Notice explained” is a separate fact from “consent given”.
Do not infer a grant from saving a profile, payment or the owner ticking a box.
When evidence is missing or refused, show that state and block the dependent
permission-requiring operation; do not repeatedly request an optional opt-in.
The final service/refusal handling remains subject to approved policy.

## Family data supply and wearer informed

Use two separate, initially unselected confirmations for a person supplying
another wearer's data. Record the actual giver and wearer, not just a family label.

1. English: “I am authorised to provide the named wearer's details for this
   tailoring-service interaction.”
   Tamil: “இந்தத் தையல் சேவை தொடர்பாக, குறிப்பிடப்பட்ட ஆடை அணிபவரின் விவரங்களை
   வழங்க எனக்கு அனுமதி உள்ளது.”
2. English: “I have informed the named wearer about the details being provided
   and their stated use.”
   Tamil: “வழங்கப்படும் விவரங்கள் மற்றும் அவை எதற்குப் பயன்படுத்தப்படும் என்பதை
   குறிப்பிடப்பட்ட ஆடை அணிபவருக்குத் தெரிவித்துள்ளேன்.”

Each confirmation needs its own recorded/unknown/declined state and attributable
evidence. It applies to this interaction and stated scope; it grants no blanket
access to measurements, photos or other family members. Neither confirmation
substitutes for the wearer's purpose-specific consent or a minor's guardian
authority and consent. If the wearer has not been informed, record pending rather
than changing the answer to pass validation.

## Separate guardian authority and consent

Age state is explicit: under 18, adult or unknown. Unknown never defaults to adult.
Do not collect a mandatory birth date, ID number, document or photo. A minor can
remain a clearly pending registration without measurements or photos.

Keep these separate fields and decisions:

- Minor wearer: the exact person and `under_18` state.
- Contact/payer: a separately identified person, if applicable.
- Proposed parent/legal guardian: a separately identified person; no self-guardianship.
- Authority claim: parent or legal guardian, scope, source and occurrence time.
- Authority verification: pending/confirmed/rejected under the eventual approved
  method, with recorder, time and minimally necessary evidence reference. A claim
  alone cannot become confirmed authority.
- Purpose decision: separate measurement and private-photo decisions, exact
  notice version/language, actual consent giver and linked authority evidence.

English authority statement: “I am the named child's parent or legal guardian.
My authority for this request has been explained and recorded separately.”

Tamil authority statement: “குறிப்பிடப்பட்ட சிறாரின் பெற்றோர் அல்லது சட்டப்பூர்வ
பாதுகாவலர் நான். இந்தக் கோரிக்கைக்கான எனது அதிகாரம் தனியாக விளக்கிப் பதிவு செய்யப்பட்டுள்ளது.”

English purpose grant: “For the named child, I agree to the stated use of the
selected information: measurements / optional private fitting photos.”

Tamil purpose grant: “குறிப்பிடப்பட்ட சிறாருக்காகத் தேர்ந்தெடுக்கப்பட்ட தகவல்களை,
கூறப்பட்ட நோக்கில் பயன்படுத்த ஒப்புக்கொள்கிறேன்: அளவுகள் / விருப்பத் தனிப்பட்ட
பொருத்தப் புகைப்படங்கள்.”

Present each purpose separately; no combined preselected checkbox. The authority
statement is not itself verification, and the owner recording it is not the
consent giver. An aunt who only pays cannot give guardian consent. Pending,
rejected, refused or withdrawn authority/consent never makes minor measurements
or photos eligible. No guardian grant can permit a minor's publication or marketing.

## Refusal, withdrawal and independent optional purposes

English actions: “I do not agree to this purpose.” / “I withdraw my earlier
consent for this purpose.”

Tamil actions: “இந்த நோக்கத்திற்கு நான் ஒப்புக்கொள்ளவில்லை.” /
“இந்த நோக்கத்திற்காக முன்பு அளித்த ஒப்புதலைத் திரும்பப் பெறுகிறேன்.”

Bind the action to a displayed subject, purpose and prior grant where applicable.
Append attributable events; do not replace prior evidence or silently delete it.
Recheck eligibility immediately for future dependent actions. Open a tracked
deletion/review request where needed, explain retention exceptions and propagate
withdrawal to future features; recording an event does not prove downstream
erasure or that an already downloaded copy has been recalled.

Maintain distinct purposes for ordinary service, marketing, promotional
publication and optional sensitive/private media. Optional purposes start
unselected/not recorded. Proposed adult-only labels:

- Marketing — “I agree to receive promotional messages through the selected
  channel.” / “தேர்ந்தெடுத்த தொடர்பு வழியில் விளம்பரச் செய்திகளைப் பெற ஒப்புக்கொள்கிறேன்.”
- Publication — “I agree to publication of the specifically identified image for
  the stated promotional purpose.” / “குறிப்பிட்ட படத்தைக் கூறப்பட்ட விளம்பர நோக்கில்
  வெளியிட ஒப்புக்கொள்கிறேன்.”
- Private fitting photo — “I agree to the identified private photo being stored
  for the stated fitting purpose; this does not permit publication.” /
  “குறிப்பிட்ட தனிப்பட்ட புகைப்படத்தைக் கூறப்பட்ட பொருத்தச் சரிபார்ப்பு நோக்கில் சேமிக்க
  ஒப்புக்கொள்கிறேன்; இதைப் பொதுவில் வெளியிட அனுமதி அளிக்கவில்லை.”

These labels remain draft and their downstream capabilities unavailable in this
registration slice. No marketing grant may be required for service. No minor
marketing/publication grant is accepted; private fitting images cannot be
published. Channel preference alone is not marketing consent. A later purpose or
notice change cannot inherit an unrelated previous grant.

## Recording contract and decisions still pending

For every applicable event retain shop, subject, purpose, immutable notice
version/content digest, language shown/explained, decision, actual giver,
recording owner, source/channel, occurrence and recording times, authority or
family-confirmation references and linked prior event. Separate a person's
statement from the owner's observation; do not invent evidence, backdate a grant
or copy sensitive text into general audit logs. Corrections append linked events.

Before live activation the owner and appropriate privacy reviewer must resolve:

- **Retention:** approve periods and triggers per data category, including consent
  evidence, drafts, audit changes, measurements/media, financial exceptions,
  exports, backups and processors. No duration is selected by this draft; no
  indefinite-retention promise or automatic production purge follows from it.
- **Grievance and requests:** name the accountable boutique contact and usable
  contact channel, review request handling and explanations, and approve the
  customer-facing text. Synthetic demonstrations say “Grievance contact pending
  owner review — real-data use blocked”; they contain no invented real contact.
- **Verification:** approve a proportionate method for identifying a requestor
  and confirming guardian authority, with disputed/unknown cases and minimal
  evidence handling. Synthetic fixtures may exercise explicit test outcomes but
  cannot establish a real person's authority. No mandatory document is invented.
- **Processors and applicability:** confirm actual services/locations, written
  processing agreement, breach procedure and applicable obligations/commencement
  with appropriate advice. Architecture lists candidates; this draft does not
  certify deployment, contracts or legal applicability.
- **Language and approval:** obtain fluent-Tamil and owner review of both texts,
  approve the final notice/guardian flow and refusal consequences, version and
  record the decision. Draft status must remain visible until that occurs.

Real-data activation also remains subject to the other product §§32–33 and TRU-10
readiness gates. Passing synthetic tests, publishing tickets or merging code does
not resolve these decisions. This document changes no product or architecture
baseline and records no production privacy approval.
