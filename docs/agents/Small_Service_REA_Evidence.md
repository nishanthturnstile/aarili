## What was actually observed

**TailorMan public sample:** the alteration surface is separate from new billing.
It presents three sample jobs with a garment/request summary, date, quoted amount
and progress. There are All, Pending, In Progress, Done and Delivered filters.
No distinct small-service/add-on editor, original-order link, draft recovery,
wearer/guardian permissions or hidden-field rule was established. New Alteration
click left the captured page unchanged, so there is no intake/save/reopen proof.
Displayed prices are vendor samples and supply no Aarili rate or capacity policy.

**Additional T3 observation, not REA Evidence:** selecting Tamil in TailorMan's
Language control changed tour narration into Tamil while the alteration list and
main controls remained English in the inspected snapshot. The narrated tour also
advanced to another screen when left running; subsequent inspection navigated
back and paused it. No business save occurred. This is bounded language behaviour,
not a full translation/accessibility evaluation.

**Nool authenticated research:** the two records created in earlier investigations
remain Pending. The saved multi-garment detail still shows independent row prices
but a shared order status and no change events. Its visible actions do not expose
Reopen for Alteration. We did not mark either order delivered, create payments,
invoice, assign workers, send messages, upload media or manufacture a qualifying
case. The public guide's delivered-order prerequisite therefore remains unmet.
We cannot report a successful reopen, its editor, changed price, linkage or
persistence from this account state.

Standalone walk-in alteration, standalone saree services and a correction to an
earlier garment are different cases. A dedicated alteration list establishes
neither the standalone create flow nor the linked-correction workflow. Nool's
guide establishes intended linked workflow, not walk-in intake or real persistence.

## Inferences, independent decisions and unknowns

**Inference:** compact garment/request/date/price rows could help an owner scan
lightweight jobs. No time-on-task or usability benefit was measured. Aarili should
reuse its own intake/detail hierarchy and conditional fields, rather than copy
vendor screens or inherit a mandatory body-measurement step.

**Independent Aarili decisions:** preserve an original accepted record and use
attributable new linked work/amendments; distinguish line completion from garment
quality/handover; retain customer-property identity without pretending a receipt;
require reviewed comparable capacity demand. These follow Aarili's sources, not
inferred competitor implementation.
